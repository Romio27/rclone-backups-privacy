rclone backups (india)

This is a private, single-user Google OAuth client used by its owner to upload backups of the owner's own servers to the owner's own Google Drive with rclone.

Privacy policy

  • The app requests only the drive.file scope: access to files the app itself created.
  • Data is transferred only between the owner's servers and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The app is not offered to other users.
  • Contact: chemeris27@gmail.com

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
